The Chronicle

Two prayers, one amen

Every good story begins quietly, and ours began in a glasshouse full of ferns — one shared umbrella, and a first conversation that outlasted the rain. We talked until the candles guttered, and somewhere between the last hymn and the first starlight, we each went home and prayed the same prayer.

Four years and two cities later, our families gathered beneath one roof to give their blessing, and the date was written in pen and prayer. Nothing would mean more than to have you standing beside us as we exchange our vows before God, and begin a home built on faith, hope and love.
